Executive Abstract & Real-Time Application

🏥 Clinical Real-Time Application

Cardiovascular disease (CVD) is the leading cause of global mortality (~17.9M annual deaths). Early risk stratification is crucial to prevent acute myocardial infarction.

Goal: Deploy an automated, real-time Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) for emergency cardiac triage using non-invasive diagnostic indicators.

🔬 Benchmarking Scope

Evaluated 8 Machine Learning algorithms across distinct mathematical families under rigorous 5-fold cross-validation:

  • Linear & Probabilistic (Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes)
  • Distance & Instance-Based (KNN)
  • Tree & Ensembles (Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting)
  • Kernel & Neural (SVM RBF, Multi-Layer Perceptron)

End-to-End Machine Learning Pipeline Workflow

1. Data Engineering
  • Median/Mode missing imputation
  • 99th percentile Winsorization (chol, trestbps)
  • One-Hot dummy encoding (drop_first=True)
2. Splitting & Scaling
  • Stratified 80:20 Train-Test split
  • StandardScaler fitted strictly on X_train
  • Zero data leakage across folds
3. Optimization & Testing
  • GridSearchCV on Random Forest
  • 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation
  • Comprehensive multi-metric evaluation

Key Inferences & Comparative Discussion

🎯 Clinical Sensitivity vs Accuracy

In medical triage, a False Negative is catastrophic (patient dies of untreated ischemia). KNN & SVM achieved 96.43% Recall (only 1 false negative out of 28 cardiac cases).

🌲 Bagging vs Single Tree

Bagging 150 regularized trees in Random Forest reduced single-tree variance, elevating Recall from 67.86% (Decision Tree) to 92.86% and Accuracy from 80.33% to 93.44%.

🧠 Neural Networks on Small Data

MLP achieved 68.85% accuracy; deep neural nets require tens of thousands of samples to surpass regularized tree ensembles on tabular clinical features.

⚡ Top Clinical Biomarkers

Top 4 drivers of cardiovascular risk identified via Gini Importance: Fluoroscopy vessel count (`ca`), Thallium defect (`thal`), ST-depression (`oldpeak`), and Max Heart Rate (`thalach`).
